# 5. People and Operations

Results depend on clear roles, good processes and teams that are trained for the work.

## Core steps

1. **Clarify roles.** For every major outcome, name who is responsible, who decides and who must be consulted.
2. **Document the key processes.** The ten processes that drive revenue, delivery and service, each on one page.
3. **Set the rhythm.** Weekly team review, monthly performance review, quarterly planning.
4. **Train and develop.** A short onboarding path, role-based training and regular practice with feedback.
5. **Improve continuously.** Collect problems at the front line, fix the top three each month.

## Signals of a healthy operation

- New people reach full productivity on a predictable schedule.
- Hand-offs between teams have owners and time limits.
- Quality, speed and cost are measured and discussed regularly.
