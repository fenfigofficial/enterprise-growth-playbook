# 2. Business Development, Sales and Marketing


Growth comes from a clear target customer, a clear offer and a repeatable way to reach and convert them.


## Core steps


1. **Define the ideal customer.** Industry, size, role of the buyer, the problem they pay to solve.
2. **Sharpen the offer.** One sentence on the result the customer gets, the proof you have, and the price logic.
3. **Choose channels.** Pick two or three channels where your buyers already pay attention and do them well.
4. **Build the pipeline.** Define stages, entry and exit rules, owners and response times.
5. **Measure.** Track leads, conversion by stage, cycle length, deal size and customer retention.


## Content that works for enterprise buyers


- Short guides that solve one problem in detail.
- Case studies with a clear before, action and result.
- Comparison pages that help a buyer decide.
- Regular, consistent publishing over occasional bursts.


## Common mistakes


- Selling to everyone instead of one defined customer.
- Reporting activity (posts, calls) instead of results (qualified opportunities, revenue).
- No follow-up process after the first contact.
