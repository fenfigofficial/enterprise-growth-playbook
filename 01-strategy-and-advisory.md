# 1. Strategy and Advisory

Strategy answers three questions: where will we compete, how will we win, and what will we stop doing.

## Core steps

1. **Diagnose.** Describe the current position in facts: customers, revenue sources, cost structure, capabilities, competitors.
2. **Choose.** Pick a small number of priorities (three to five). Write down what you will not do.
3. **Size the opportunity.** Estimate market, share, investment and payback with stated assumptions.
4. **Plan the path.** Turn each priority into initiatives with an owner, a budget, milestones and a measure.
5. **Review.** Meet every quarter, compare results with the plan, and change the plan when the facts change.

## Questions leadership should be able to answer

- Who is our most valuable customer and why do they choose us?
- Which three numbers tell us the business is healthy?
- What is the single biggest risk to the plan, and who owns it?
- Which investment gives the best return over the next 12 months?

## Common mistakes

- Too many priorities, so nothing gets resourced properly.
- Targets with no owner or no date.
- Treating the plan as a document instead of a monthly management routine.
