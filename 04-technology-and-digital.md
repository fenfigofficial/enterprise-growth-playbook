# 4. Technology and Digital

Technology should remove work, improve decisions and make the customer experience simpler. Start with the business problem, then choose the tool.

## Core steps

1. **Map the processes.** Find the steps that are manual, repeated or error-prone.
2. **Prioritize.** Score each opportunity by value, effort and risk. Start with high value and low risk.
3. **Design the architecture.** Keep systems modular, integrate through clear interfaces, and keep one source of truth for data.
4. **Deliver in small releases.** Ship usable improvements every few weeks and learn from real use.
5. **Secure and govern.** Access control, backups, monitoring and a clear owner for every system.

## Principles

- Integrate before you replace.
- Automate the routine, keep humans on exceptions and judgment.
- Measure adoption, not only delivery.
