# 3. Brand and Experience

A brand is a promise that is kept in every interaction. Consistency builds trust faster than any campaign.

## Core steps

1. **Purpose and positioning.** Why the organization exists and what it stands for, in two sentences.
2. **Identity.** Name, logo, colours, typography and imagery used the same way everywhere.
3. **Voice.** How the brand speaks: clear, confident, respectful. Write examples of what to say and what to avoid.
4. **Experience.** Map the journey from first contact to renewal and fix the moments that create friction.
5. **Governance.** One owner, one source of truth for assets, and a simple approval routine.

## Checklist for every touchpoint

- Is the logo used at the right size and with enough space?
- Do colours and fonts match the guidelines?
- Does the message say one clear thing?
- Is the next step for the customer obvious?
